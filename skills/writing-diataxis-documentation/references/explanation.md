# Explanation

Need: deepen understanding through study and reflection, usually away from the
immediate work.

- Begin with a bounded "why" or "tell me about" question. Explanation has no
  natural boundary of its own.
- Make connections. Supply context, causes, constraints, history, implications,
  alternatives, counterexamples, and analogies where they illuminate the topic.
- Admit reasoned opinion and perspective. Understanding can require comparison
  of different positions.
- Discuss the subject. Keep action and catalogs of machinery in their own forms.
- Link procedures and reference facts instead of absorbing them.

Reject a draft whose primary use is consultation during a task or whose topic is
too open-ended to reach a coherent understanding.
